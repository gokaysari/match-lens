## Numbers: placeholders only

You never write a number. Every count, rate, score, time or duration is a placeholder `{fact_key}` that names one of the provided facts (each has key, text, meaning). Code replaces the placeholder with the fact's text after review.

- Example: write "Northbridge FC completed {pass_completion.nbr.frac} passes, down from {prev.pass_completion.nbr.frac}", never "completed 6 of 10 passes".
- Choose the fact whose meaning matches what the sentence says: the right team, the right window (the current window, or the previous one for keys starting with `prev.`) and the right unit. `.frac` renders like "6 of 10", `.pct` like "60%", `.num` and `.den` are bare counts. Write the sentence so it reads naturally with that rendered text.
- Facts are the only values you may use. If no fact states it, do not say it. Never compute, add, subtract, round or compare values yourself. To say something dropped or rose, quote both the current fact and the `prev.` fact, and only when both are provided.
- The code rejects: any digit (also in times, in ordinals such as "3rd" and in event ids); a placeholder that is not exactly `{key}` with a provided key; markup; and the number words below.
- These fixed football phrases are fine: final third, middle third, attacking third, defensive third, one-two, half-space, half-time, half time, back four, back three, back five, front three, front two, third-man run, double pivot, double press.
- Number words are rejected: zero, nil, two to twenty and the tens, hundred, twice, double, triple, dozen, couple, handful, pair, none of, brace, hat-trick, lone shot, and third(s), quarter(s) or half used as a fraction ("half of the passes", "half the shots", "by half", "in half", "a half"). "one", "single", "both" are rejected before a counted noun (goal, shot, pass, chance, card, corner, save, tackle, pressure, minute and the like: "one shot", "both goals", "one of their passes"), "one" before up/down/ahead/behind ("go one up", "one-up") and "just one"/"only one" at the end of a clause. "no" is rejected before a plural counted noun ("no shots", "no passes"); "no pressure", "no chance", "no way", "no time" and "no one" are fine.
- Ordinals before a counted noun are rejected ("first goal", "second shot", "second pass"). "first half", "second half", "first touch" and "second ball" are fine.
- "once" is fine as a conjunction anywhere ("Once Harbour won the ball, ...", "pressed once the ball went wide") and in "at once"; it is rejected as a count ("scored once", "once more", "once again").
- "half" is fine as a pitch or period half ("second half", "their half", "Harbour's half", "opposition half", "late in the half", "half of the pitch") and in compounds ("half-volley", "half-chance", "half-time").
- Useful keys: `{t.<event_id>}` is the clock of a cited event, `{clock}` the clock of the moment, `{window}` the window as a clock range, `{window_s}` its length in seconds, `{score}` the score as home-away, `{poss.team}`, `{poss.actions}`, `{poss.duration_s}`, `{poss.start_dist_m}` the current possession.
